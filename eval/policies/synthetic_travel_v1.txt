1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs reimbursement of business travel and related expenses for all full-time employees of the company. It applies to domestic and international travel undertaken at the request of the company. Contract staff are covered only when their engagement letter says so. Where a local law sets a stricter limit, the local law prevails over this policy.

2. General Principles

Employees must spend company money as carefully as they would their own. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip. Claims submitted after 90 days will not be reimbursed under any circumstances. Every claim must be supported by an itemized original receipt, except where this policy explicitly allows a self-declaration. Personal expenses such as minibar charges, in-room movies, spa services and laundry for trips shorter than four nights are never reimbursable.

3. Meals

Meal expenses in metro cities are limited to Rs. 1500 per day, inclusive of taxes and tips. In non-metro cities the meal limit is Rs. 1000 per day. Grade L5 and above may claim up to Rs. 2500 per day for meals in any city. Alcohol is not reimbursable as part of a meal claim. Business meals with clients are covered separately under section 8 and do not count against the daily meal limit. When a meal is included in the hotel tariff or the conference fee, no separate meal allowance may be claimed for that meal.

4. Lodging

Lodging for Grade L1 to L3 employees in metro cities is capped at Rs. 6000 per night. In non-metro cities the lodging cap for these grades is Rs. 4000 per night. Grade L4 employees may stay at hotels up to Rs. 8000 per night in metro cities. Grade L5 and above stay at actuals in company-approved hotels. All hotel bookings must be made through the corporate travel desk. A booking made outside the travel desk requires written approval from the department head before the stay. Staying with friends or family instead of a hotel earns a flat allowance of Rs. 1200 per night with no receipt required.

5. Local Transport

Ride-share and taxi transport is capped at Rs. 800 per trip within city limits. Airport transfers are reimbursed at actuals and are exempt from the per-trip cap. Use of a personal car is reimbursed at Rs. 12 per kilometre, and the employee must record the start and end odometer readings. Parking and tolls are reimbursed at actuals with receipts. Self-drive rental cars require pre-approval from the travel desk.

6. Air and Rail Travel

Domestic flights must be booked in economy class at least 14 days in advance where the travel date is known. Grade L5 and above may fly business class on flights longer than four hours. Rail travel in AC 2-tier is permitted for all grades; AC first class is permitted for L4 and above. Cancellation charges are reimbursed only when the trip was cancelled for a business reason documented by the manager.

7. International Travel

International travel requires pre-approval from a vice president at least 21 days before departure. A per diem of USD 75 per day covers meals and incidentals for international trips, in place of the domestic meal limits in section 3. International travel meal caps are waived entirely for approved business trips exceeding five days; on such trips meals are reimbursed at actuals. Travel insurance is purchased centrally by the company and must not be claimed by employees. Visa fees and passport photographs are reimbursable; passport renewal fees are not.

8. Client Entertainment

Client entertainment requires prior approval from the employee's manager. The limit is Rs. 3000 per guest per event, with a maximum of Rs. 15000 per event. The names and organisations of all guests must be listed on the claim. Gifts to government officials are prohibited in all circumstances.

9. Exceptions

9.1 A department head may approve a one-time exception of up to 20 percent above any domestic cap in this policy, with written justification attached to the claim.
9.2 During declared emergencies such as floods or civil unrest, lodging caps are suspended and employees should choose safe accommodation.
9.3 Grade L3 employees travelling internationally follow the international per diem in section 7 and not the standard meal cap.

10. Approvals and Audit

Claims above Rs. 50000 in total require approval from the finance controller in addition to the manager. The finance team audits a random ten percent sample of claims each quarter. Falsifying a receipt is a disciplinary offence that may lead to termination.
