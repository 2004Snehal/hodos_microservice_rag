## B. Daily Allowances Guidelines for Business Travel — Domestic and International

1. Travellers shall be paid Daily Allowances on a per diem basis for covering meal expenses and other miscellaneous expenses (eg tips), Daily Allowances shall be of 2 types — Meal Allowances and Overnight Allowances.
2. Meal Allowances shall be payable to an employee, while on a business travel, in case the stay / accommodation is provided / reimbursed by the company. Overnight Allowances shall be payable to an employee, while on a business travel, in case the stay / accommodation is managed by the employee and not reimbursed by the company.
3. If the Employee has business meals with any person/s for breakfast, lunch or dinner in the course of business while on tour, s/he can claim the actual expenses incurred on such expenditure subject to approvals.

## B.1 Guidelines on Daily Allowances

## 1. Meal / Daily allowances will be paid for all meals & miscellaneous expense. The reduction of any meal allowances will no longer be applicable.

2. On occasions, where COMPANY XX / any other third party is making arrangements/reimbursing separately for all meals - such as offsites, conferences and includes business meals with employees/external clients, per diem allowances cannot be claimed.
3. On the last day of a continuous tour, if the employee checks out of the Hotel / Guest House in the morning and returns to the base location after completion of her/his work later in the day, s/he would be eligible for allowances in line with "Meal Allowance" for that day. On the day of departure or arrival of a continuous trip, "Meal Allowance" shall be payable at 100% rate (For more travel more than 6 hours) and 50% rate for less than 6 hours. "Overnight Allowance" will not be applicable be applicable during travel and/or transit duration.
4. Incase of one day business trip / Short trips, employee would be eligible for allowances in line with "Meal Allowance" for that day
5. Anny tips paid during the course of the tour are covered in the daily allowances and no separate claims can be made on this account.
6. For the day of departure and on the day of arrival, allowance as applicable to the place from where the journey commences will be paid.
7. Start time of the tour to be considered by an employee as leaving time from place of work or residence, similarly arrival time from tour will be considered as reaching time at the place of work or residence.
